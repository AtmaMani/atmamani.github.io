Title: 2024 International Institute of Business Analysis - Orange County Chapter

## Title: Cracking the Code: Agile Process Management Demystified

**About the event**: IIBA is a professional association dedicated to supporting business analysis professionals, providing resources, and fostering community. I was invited to speak about Agile Process and how that can be applied from a practitioner's perspective. I spoke about some history - how the manufacturing and software world was before agile, then transitioned into the core of agile and the ceremonies associated with it.

Link to the session: [IIBA: Cracking the Code - Atma Mani](https://oc-ca.iiba.org/events/atma-mani-cracking-the-code-agile-process-management-demystified/)
